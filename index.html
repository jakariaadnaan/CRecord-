    How to record a call

    1. Choose the contact, or type a name.
    2. Start or answer the call and turn on speakerphone.
    3. Tap the red button. Keep this app open and the screen on.
    4. Tap the button again when the call ends. The recording is saved automatically.

    Tell the other person

    Let them know the call is being recorded. Many places require their consent. You are responsible for following the law where you live.
